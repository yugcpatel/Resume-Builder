Software Development Student (Acoustics)

From space cameras to nuclear reactors to autonomous vehicles and sophisticated medical devices, QNX provides the foundation operating system that runs these systems.

The QNX Acoustics Team is where world-class engineering meets real-world impact. If you're passionate about audio technology, signal processing, and real-time embedded systems, this is a place where your work directly shapes the sound experience in millions of vehicles and devices worldwide. You'll collaborate with experts across acoustics, machine learning, and system design to help build high-performance solutions trusted by global OEMs. It's a team that values curiosity, technical depth, and hands-on innovation, giving you the freedom to push boundaries while working on products that truly matter.
