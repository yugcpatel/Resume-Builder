We are looking for Software Developer interns for our growing team!



What you'll do here:

Work in the development teams building new features for our products Harbour (Internal Developer Platform), Mentor (Opinionated CICD Pipeline), IT-Health (Software Metrics and Analytics) and Agora (Knowledge Base and Community Engagement Platform).

Support the team in triaging and fixing production issues.

Work with a Product Centric Team for developing, testing, and getting feedback.

Freedom to bring your own ideas and solutions and follow best practices in a small, agile and fun team.

Technical Skills required: Java, GitHub

Nice to have: Spring Boot, Experience Building Rest Services, API Scraping, MongoDB, AWS


What you bring to the table:

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in a field related to software development, computer science, or anything relevant;

Available to work with us full-time, 35 hours per week, for the Fall 2026 Term (September to December)

Must be an active student during your internship and/or returning to school in next Winter 2027 following your internship

No Canadian work experience is required but must be eligible to work in Canada.

Understand the software development lifecycle and be curious about continuous delivery pipelines and testing.

Strong analytical abilities and problem-solving skills.

Adaptability, curiosity, and commitment to continuous learning.

Excellent communication skills with a passion for technology.

Self-reliant with a strong sense of responsibility.
