POSITION SUMMARY

SFU's WearTech Labs is seeking a motivated Research Support Intern for a 4 to 8 month term. In this role you will support research and development for products related to physiology, performance, and wearable technologies. As part of our multidisciplinary team, you may contribute to research related tasks, including but not limited to, project conceptualization, experimental design, data collection and analysis, software and hardware prototyping, and report writing for both academic and industry-facing initiatives. In addition to this, you may contribute to facility management tasks such as equipment maintenance, and supplies management.



DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Research Development and Execution:

Assist in the planning and execution of studies related to physiology, performance, and wearable technologies.
Collect and manage data using laboratory systems (e.g., 3D motion capture, force plates, metabolic carts, etc.).
Assist with the development and refinement of experimental protocols.
Conduct data processing, statistical analysis, and interpretation.
Assist with software development for data acquisition, processing, or visualization pipelines.
Contribute to prototyping and fabrication, including 3D printing and basic mechanical assembly.
Generate reports and documentation to summarize existing literature, outline project progress, and communicate results.
Equipment Maintenance

Perform equipment calibrations and maintenance following specified protocols.
Assist with troubleshooting and resolving system malfunctions.
Contribute to the creation and updating of equipment manuals and procedural guides.
Support supplies management.


QUALIFICATIONS

Required

Completed courses in biomechanics, physiology, engineering, or related fields.
Interest in interdisciplinary research, particularly in performance, physiology, and wearable technologies.
Demonstrated ability to use AI tools effectively and critically.
Demonstrated excellent attention to detail.
Demonstrated excellent communication skills, both oral and written.
A GPA of 80% or greater.
Preferred

Familiarity with physiological and biomechanical metrics and data acquisition systems.
Working knowledge of Python, Matlab, or similar tools for data processing and analysis.
Experience writing technical summaries, academic reports, or research documentation.
Experience with CAD software.
Experience with basic robotics/electronics.
Demonstrated ability to self-learn.
A GPA of 90% or greater.
